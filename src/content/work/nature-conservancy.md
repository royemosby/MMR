summary: "Early-stage digital strategy can create a peculiar kind of risk: everyone agrees the current experience needs to change while holding very different ideas about what the new one should become. I led this engagement from an initially broad mandate through wireframes, prototypes, user testing, refinement, and final handoff."
visibleProblem: "The team needed to learn without letting every possibility become the strategy. The engagement had to balance two useful but competing modes of thinking: one expansive (what could the experience become?), the other practical (what belongs in a credible first version, and what evidence would tell us if the direction was right?)."
hiddenSystemIssue: "Without a structure to convert ambitious thinking into something specific enough to evaluate, every open question threatened to delay the direction. The project needed a way to preserve possibility while continuously narrowing it toward a decision the organization could act on."
stakes: "A digital experience direction for one of the world's largest conservation organizations — with leadership alignment, internal usability consensus, and external user evidence all required before the final handoff."
operatingIntervention: "I built and managed the structure connecting strategy to evidence: translating broad goals into concrete experience questions; maintaining the distinction between MVP direction and longer-term possibilities; coordinating wireframing and prototype development; structuring internal and external usability testing; managing feedback and revision cycles; connecting research findings back to design decisions; and coordinating content-model and governance considerations alongside the experience work. External usability testing reached its full planned participant target."
whatChanged: "The final deliverable was not simply a set of wireframes. The organization had a more defined version of the experience, evidence from real users about how that direction performed, and a clearer distinction between what belonged in the immediate path forward and what remained a future possibility."
leadershipLesson: "The point of research was not to delay the decision. It was to make the decision better."
