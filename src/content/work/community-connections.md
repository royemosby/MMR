summary: "I led Community Connections, an approximately $1 million multiyear statewide referral-platform initiative sponsored by Children's Trust of South Carolina. The platform could create a new pathway for families and practitioners to find and connect with services. The harder question was what had to exist around the technology for people to actually use it."
visibleProblem: "Providers needed to maintain accurate information. Partner organizations needed to understand where the platform fit into their work. Families needed a clear place to begin. Agencies and implementation partners needed a shared approach to rollout, communication, training, and adoption. The technology could create a new pathway. It could not create those conditions by itself."
hiddenSystemIssue: "The initiative was structured as a technology deployment, but the real work was treating communications, partner participation, provider data, training, and adoption as implementation dependencies — not secondary activities. Without that framing, adoption would stall regardless of platform quality."
stakes: "A statewide public-service platform funded by state and federal sources, serving families and practitioners across South Carolina — with adoption success tied to data quality, partner participation, and an operating model that could sustain the network after launch."
operatingIntervention: "As overall project lead, I coordinated agency partners, the technology vendor, internal stakeholders, and a two-person implementation and communications team. I led the phased statewide rollout, partner engagement and coordination, vendor management, onboarding and training planning, adoption planning and measurement, the public-facing user journey, and development of scParents as the entry point and communications layer around the underlying resource network."
whatChanged: "By the time I transitioned off during the first year of rollout, the initiative had established a clearer public journey, a partner-engagement model, training and onboarding structures, adoption measures, and an operating approach designed to support the resource network over time."
leadershipLesson: "Implementation is not the moment a system becomes available. It is the work of creating the conditions that allow people to use it."
