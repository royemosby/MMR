operatingIntervention: "Rebuilt the operating layer: clarified decision rights, established a weekly rhythm of shared visibility, created explicit risk tracking, and gave stakeholders a reliable narrative about what was happening and why."
whatChanged: "Delivery velocity increased. Stakeholder trust recovered. The launch succeeded on revised timelines with full organizational alignment."
leadershipLesson: "When strategy is stuck, the problem is almost never the strategy itself. It is the missing operating layer between intent and execution."

The work was not the problem. The operating structure around the work was.
